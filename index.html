The pattern

Three projects. Same order, every time.

Redefine the problem → make it run → leave a system behind. This is the throughline of everything below.

2023–2025 · Ears for Scent

A perfume made of memories

“Elderly isolation” → “No one listens to their stories.”

We interviewed seniors, rewrote their memories as postcards, and captured them in a scent — our first product, 《Madeleine》. Crowdfunded at 112% of goal with ₩0 ad budget, then we went back and taught the same seniors to write AI poetry.

2025–2026 · Nova Robotics

The dataset that didn't exist

“Urban flooding” → “Which drain do we clear first?”

Cities can clean ~300 drains a day; Korea has 4.37 million. No public data told us which ones mattered — so we walked the streets and built the dataset ourselves. As proposal & data lead I wrote 10 winning applications in 58 days and the plan that secured a ₩60M government grant.

2026 · Moduus AX

The job I automated was my own

“Repetitive work” → “Rules waiting to be encoded.”

I was hired for my slide-making. So I audited 83 real work conversations, extracted the rules, and taught them to 66 Claude Code agents. Proposal drafting fell from 8 hours to 1 — and my job moved up a level. Nobody asked me to; that's the point.
